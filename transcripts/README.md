## 🎧 Audio Quality, Anomalies & Production Notes

### 1. Acoustic Resonance & Reverberation
- **Contestant-3:** Exhibited higher room acoustic reverberation and variable mic distance. All phonetically ambiguous words were honestly documented using `[unclear]` and `[possibly: WORD]` tags to maintain 100% transcript integrity without inventing dialogue.
- **Contestant-4 & Contestant-11:** Extremely rapid, high-energy delivery by field reporters and anchors. Slips of the tongue and phonetic truncations were preserved verbatim with explanatory bracketed annotations (e.g., `*[intended text]*`).

### 2. Standby Transitions & Countdown Timing
- **Contestant-2:** Had an extended pre-broadcast transition window (`02:05 - 03:12`), starting the broadcast slightly later than the typical ~03:00 mark.
- **Contestant-8:** Ran for a full `05:27` competition broadcast window (`02:50 - 08:17`), concluding with an emphatic unison sign-off (*"Offline!"*).
- **Contestant-10 & Contestant-17:** Feature explicit floor manager and studio countdown cues (e.g., *"30 seconds silence on set"*, *"20 seconds"*, *"15 seconds"*, *"10, 9, 8, 7..."*) during the pre-broadcast standby phase.

### 3. Creative Mic Check Formats
- **Contestant-6:** Conducted an entertaining mock podcast (*"Info Hacks with Ria and Faye Podcast"*) during the mic check testing window.
- **Contestant-13:** Rehearsed an emotional drama letter-reading skit (*"Dear Kuya Will... Ako po si Gualterio, 100 years old"*) before switching to the hard-news competitive format.
- **Contestant-15:** Practiced a food critique show (*"Rate Mo, Pitig Ko"*) and comedy banter before launching into their official station OBB theme song.

### 4. Special Reconstructions & Quality Fixes
- **Contestant-10:** Initially misidentified as English due to automated language tagging; fully re-transcribed with Whisper `large-v3` (`language: tl`) to capture the authentic Filipino broadcast dialogue.
- **Contestant-14 & Contestant-18:** Resolved Whisper repetition loops during low-level audio/music beds, restoring complete verbatim transcriptions for all opening spiels, top headlines, and initial news reports.
