# Specialist M — Competition Quality

**Benchmark:** NSPC (National Schools Press Conference) Radio Broadcasting & Scriptwriting — 18 national-level entries (Contestant-1 … Contestant-18).
**Sources read (primary):** all 18 `transcripts/Contestant-N/actual-script.md` files (full), plus `corpus-inventory.md` and `ANALYSIS-SPEC.md`. `mic-check.md` files were **not** analyzed, per spec.
**Question:** What does the corpus show about competition quality — preparation, polish, originality, clarity, cohesion, timing, technical sophistication, script economy, delivery support, memorability — and which entries show the strongest script craft?

**Evidence labels:** `DIRECTLY OBSERVED` (explicit in a script, with quote) · `CROSS-SCRIPT PATTERN` (seen in multiple named entries) · `ANALYST INFERENCE` (interpretation/judgment, with reasoning). Quoted lines are verbatim; bracketed `[intended text]` / `*[intended text]*` / `[possibly: …]` / `[unclear]` annotations are the editors' reconstructions of mis-spoken or unclear audio and are quoted exactly as they appear. Entries are abbreviated C1–C18.

### A9. Delivery support (pronunciation-friendly writing)

**What survives fast Filipino/Taglish delivery.** `DIRECTLY OBSERVED`:
