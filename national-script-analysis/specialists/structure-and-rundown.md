## 3. Segment Ordering Across All 18

### 3.1 The invariant core (CROSS-SCRIPT PATTERN, 18/18)

Every entry, without exception, follows this seven-position spine:

```
OPENING → HARD NEWS → TEASER/BUMPER → INFOMERCIAL → RE-ENTRY → SOFT NEWS → CLOSING
```

- **Opening first:** all 18 begin with a station-branded open (OBB theme, station ID/jingle, anchor greeting, headline teasers) before the first full report.
- **Hard news before the break:** all 18 place 2–3 hard-news reports before the infomercial. The Ormoc City ATM-scam story and the West Philippine Sea cyanide story appear in 14/18 and 15/18 entries respectively (CROSS-SCRIPT PATTERN — shared news-day content, e.g., C1: *"Timbog sa ikinasang operasyon ng Ormoc City Cyber Response Team ang isang scammer…"*; C7: *"Pinaiting [Pinaigting] ng NSC ang pagbabantay sa West Philippine Sea matapos makakuha ang mga sundan [sundalo] ng Pilipino ng 10 bote ng cyanide…"*).
- **Exactly one infomercial, mid-broadcast:** 18/18 contain exactly one infomercial/PSA, always after the hard-news block and before the soft block.
- **Break teaser before the infomercial:** 17/18 announce the return with a teaser-plus-promise line. Representative DIRECTLY OBSERVED quotes: C7 *"Huwag kayong aalis, kami ay magbabalik!"*; C10 *"Manatiling nakatutok at kami'y magbabalik pagkatapos ng isang paalala."*; C11 *"Magbabalik kami pagkatapos ng isang paalala."*; C16 *"Up next sa pagbabalik! Susunod! … Tutok lang sa NSPC Patrol!"* Only C12 enters the infomercial with a bare audio bed (DIRECTLY OBSERVED: segment 05 begins `[AUDIO: INFOMERCIAL JINGLE / SOUND EFFECTS IN]` with no spoken bumper).
- **Re-entry after the infomercial:** 13/18 have an explicit spoken or voiced re-entry (C2, C3, C4, C7, C9, C10, C11, C13, C14, C15, C16, C17, C18); C1, C6, C8 use audio-only re-entry (stinger/bed); C5 and C12 have no re-entry moment at all — C5 goes straight into anchor banter, C12 straight into a local-news teaser (DIRECTLY OBSERVED: C5 segment 7 begins *"12 pa lang ng hapon pero nag-relapse ka na."*; C12 shows no re-entry line between the infomercial extro at 05:32 and the teaser at 05:40). DIRECTLY OBSERVED spoken examples: C10 *"Nagbabalik ang NSPC Patrol!"* (06:08); C13 *"Nagbabalik ang SPC Patrol."* (06:08); C15 *"At muli pong nagbabalik ang NSPC Patrola."* (06:20); C18 *"At kami po ay nagbabalik!"* (06:19–06:20).
- **Soft block after re-entry:** sports and/or showbiz follow the infomercial in 17/18 (C7 has sports only).
- **Closing last:** all 18 end with a recap line, anchor self-identification, station identity/slogan, and outro music fade.

### 3.2 Ordering flexibility inside the blocks

**Hard-news order (local vs national/world first):**

**News-block size:** 2 stories in 13 entries (C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C8, C9, C10, C11, C14, C15, C17, C18); 3 stories in 5 entries (C6, C7, C12, C13, C16). DIRECTLY OBSERVED, e.g., C16 rundown segment 07: *"National News: West Philippine Sea Cyanide Fishing | Angel Dapito & Anchor 1 | 06:23 - 06:54"*. This is a flexible, not universal, position.

**Sports–showbiz order:** where both exist (13/18: C1–C5, C8–C11, C14, C15, C17, C18):

Sports-first is the dominant order (10/13 = 77%), but showbiz-first occurs in 3 entries, so order is a flexible element.

### 9.2 Architecture narrative

The broadcast is a **two-act structure with a commercial pivot** (ANALYST INFERENCE, derived from 18/18 rundowns):

- **Act 1 (≈0:00–2:45): Earn credibility.** Branding open → 2–3 hard-news reports with named reporters and sign-offs. The news is the product; the open sells the brand.
- **Pivot (≈2:45–3:30): The infomercial.** A single dramatized PSA, bookended by a "we'll be back" promise and a "we're back" re-entry. It functions as the mandatory content element that separates the hard from the soft (ANALYST INFERENCE: it also demonstrates advocacy/scriptwriting range, which is a judged category of the event per corpus context, though no official criteria exist in the workspace).
- **Act 2 (≈3:30–4:30): Shift tone.** Sports and/or showbiz, each with its own bed and reporter. The program relaxes without losing the station frame.
- **Coda (≈4:30–5:00): Complete the loop.** Recap line, anchor names, station slogan, fade. The broadcast ends on the same identity it opened with (CROSS-SCRIPT PATTERN: station ID appears at both ends in all 18 entries).

### 9.3 Universal structure (18/18 — DIRECTLY OBSERVED / CROSS-SCRIPT PATTERN)

1. **Branded open** — station ID + greeting + headlines, always before the first report.
2. **≥2 hard-news reports** before any break.
3. **Exactly one mid-broadcast infomercial**, after hard news, before soft news.
4. **Return promise + re-entry** around the infomercial.
5. **Post-break soft block** (sports and/or showbiz).
6. **Recap + anchor IDs + station identity + music fade close.**
7. **~5-minute total duration** (04:40–05:27 across all 18), all entries running the same 8–11 segment skeleton.
